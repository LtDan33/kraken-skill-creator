# Candidate rules

Problems the official skill-creator's pass found that no checklist rule covers. A candidate becomes a checklist rule once it shows up in audits of two different skills. How these get here: references/cross-check.md, "Learn from the gaps".

Format: date - skill audited - what failed in the official pass - the rule that would have caught it - seen in (count of distinct skills).
